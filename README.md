# TrendSpark AI

Build a production-grade AI-powered Indian swing trading platform called “TrendMatrix AI”.

The platform must look futuristic, institutional-grade, AI-first, and premium like a combination of Bloomberg Terminal + TradingView + advanced AI operating systems.

IMPORTANT:

This platform is ONLY for:

- Swing trading

- Positional trading

- Indian stock market (NSE/BSE)

NOT intraday trading.

==================================================

CORE STRATEGY PHILOSOPHY

==================================================

The AI system should focus on this core idea:

“Strong long-term monthly uptrend + clean weekly consolidation + sector-wide synchronization = high probability swing trading opportunity.”

The AI must detect:

- Strong MONTHLY trends

- WEEKLY consolidations

- Sector-wide accumulation

- Historical breakout similarities

- Relative strength

- Volume accumulation

- Institutional behavior

The AI should think like:

“When many stocks from the same strong sector begin consolidating together on weekly timeframe while maintaining bullish monthly trends, there is high probability of a sector-wide rally beginning.”

==================================================

MULTI TIMEFRAME LOGIC — HIGHEST PRIORITY

==================================================

MONTHLY TIMEFRAME = TREND VERIFICATION

The MONTHLY timeframe is used ONLY for:

- Long-term trend direction

- Sector leadership

- Institutional accumulation trend

- Macro trend confirmation

- Multi-year structure validation

The AI must prefer:

- Strong monthly uptrends

- Higher highs + higher lows

- Long-term relative strength

- Multi-month accumulation structures

- Sector leaders

Reject:

- Weak monthly structures

- Long-term downtrends

- Broken macro structures

The MONTHLY timeframe should answer:

“Is this stock in a strong long-term bullish trend?”

==================================================

WEEKLY TIMEFRAME = CONSOLIDATION + ENTRY LOGIC

==================================================

The WEEKLY timeframe is the MOST IMPORTANT execution timeframe.

The AI must use WEEKLY charts to detect:

- Tight consolidations

- Multi-week bases

- Volatility contraction

- Sideways accumulation

- Compression zones

- Cup & handle structures

- Support/resistance tightening

- Breakout pressure zones

- Volume dry-up before breakout

The WEEKLY timeframe should answer:

“Is this stock preparing for a breakout?”

==================================================

FINAL CORE LOGIC

==================================================

The AI should ONLY highly rank setups where:

1. MONTHLY trend is bullish

AND

2. WEEKLY timeframe is consolidating

This is EXTREMELY IMPORTANT.

The AI should behave like:

“Finding strong long-term trending stocks temporarily consolidating before their next major expansion.”
